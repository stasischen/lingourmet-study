怎麼試用 / What to try

讀課文、切換翻譯、點詞看讀音與意思，再讀教學解說、練習及複習。每節都有「本節詞彙」入口，可把詞或句子加入複習卡。
Read the lesson, toggle translations, open words for readings and meanings, then explore explanations, practice and review. Each section has a vocabulary list; save words or sentences as review cards.

可以故意選錯、重試、切換教學語言，再重新整理確認進度。進度只存於目前瀏覽器；清除網站資料可能使它消失。
Try a wrong answer, retry, switch explanation languages, then reload to check progress. Progress stays in this browser; clearing site data may remove it.

口說與寫作使用自我檢核，不是自動語言評分。聲音目前由瀏覽器朗讀，並非正式錄音；部分裝置可能沒有日文聲音。
Speaking and writing use self-checks, not automatic language scoring. Audio currently uses browser speech rather than recordings; some devices may have no Japanese voice.

此處尚未提供韓文、泰文或英文作為目標語的互動課程。
Interactive Korean, Thai and English courses are not available here yet.

瀏覽器資料可能包含你輸入的自我介紹及歷史副本。「全部重新開始」不會清除歷史；如需移除，請在課內開啟「已儲存的資料」，明確確認清除此節資料。
Browser data may include your typed introduction and archived copies. Starting over does not erase history. To remove it, open Saved data inside the lesson and explicitly confirm clearing that section.
